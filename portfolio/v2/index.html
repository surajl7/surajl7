Touch Screen Based Robotic Vehicle

A robotic vehicle controlled via a touch screen display for remote operation. The vehicle is equipped with an Ultrasonic sensor, Metal Detector, and Flame Sensor. RF signals from the touch display remote (range up to 20 m) are decoded at the receiver to control the motor driver IC.

Technologies: Embedded C, Arduino UNO, Circuit Design, GUI Team: 3 Year: 2020

Contactless Thermal Scanner & Automatic Sanitizer Dispenser with Pulse Oximeter

Built during the COVID-19 pandemic — a contactless thermal scanner combined with an automatic sanitizer dispenser and pulse oximeter to enable safe, touch-free health monitoring.

Technologies: Electronic Circuit Design, Embedded C, Arduino UNO Team: 3 Year: 2020

Bluetooth Controlled Robotic Vehicle

An Android app controls the robot's motion over long distances via Bluetooth. The ATMEGA328P-PU microcontroller interfaces with the Bluetooth module through UART; the robot responds to commands from the app written in Embedded C.

Technologies: Embedded C, Arduino UNO, Electronic Circuit Design Team: 3 Year: 2020
